Privacy

GitIgnore is designed to collect as little as possible.

What we store

Nothing about you. The service holds only a curated set of .gitignore templates that are compiled into the binary at build time. No personal information is stored or served.

Accounts

There are no user accounts, registration, or login of any kind. All endpoints are public.

Cookies

Two optional, non-tracking cookies may be set for your convenience:

  • theme — remembers your dark / light / auto preference.
  • lang — remembers your language selection.

Both are first-party, contain no identifying data, and can be cleared at any time without affecting access.

Logs

Operators may keep standard HTTP access logs for reliability and abuse prevention. These are controlled by whoever runs this instance, not by the application authors.
